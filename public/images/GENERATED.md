# AERION campaign images

Generated with the built-in Imagegen tool on 2026-10-09, using the actual blue
Car Concept model in the AERION road screenshot as the visual reference.
Original images are preserved locally; responsive 640/1280 WebP deliveries
are served by the site. Previous campaign JPGs remain available in the repo.

Prompt set:
- Showroom: preserve the reference cobalt blue low coupe, compact rounded
  nose, swept cyan lights, wheel arches, black five-spoke wheels and glass
  canopy; dark concrete showroom at dusk; person visible inside; diffused
  light; car on the right and quiet space on the left; no text/UI/logos.
- Cabin: same car identity, panoramic glass, ivory and graphite seats,
  no steering wheel; seated passenger speaking naturally; integrated wide
  cyan dashboard with abstract waveform/navigation; blue-hour coast;
  realistic anatomy/materials; no text/UI overlays/logos.
- Road AI: same identity; rear-seat over-shoulder view, no steering wheel;
  wide integrated dashboard; two cars on coastal highway outside; blue
  hour and embedded sensor-navigation visual; no text/branding/overlays.

These are fictional campaign interpretations of the 3D model, not exact
screenshots of the interactive scene.
