# Engine audio

Racing car engine sound loops — domasx2, CC0.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops
Original file: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/loop_0.wav
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Modified for AERION: DC removal, 50 ms smoothstep overlap at the loop boundary and peak normalization to 0.70. The edited loop lasts 0.814 seconds; playback pitch and gain respond smoothly to simulated speed. A low-pass filter and master compressor control harshness and peaks.
Electric mode uses original procedural Web Audio sound design.
